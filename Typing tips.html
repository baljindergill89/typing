⌨️ Typing Tips to Improve Your Skills

Learn how to type faster, more accurately, and efficiently!

1. Use the Correct Finger Placement

Place your fingers on the home row: A S D F (left hand) and J K L ; (right hand). Your thumbs rest on the spacebar.

2. Eyes on the Screen

Practice touch typing by focusing on the screen rather than the keyboard to improve speed and build muscle memory.

3. Maintain Good Posture

Keep your back straight, shoulders relaxed, and elbows bent at 90°. This reduces fatigue and improves accuracy.

4. Focus on Accuracy First

Typing fast is great, but accuracy comes first. It's better to type slow and correct than fast and wrong.

5. Use All Your Fingers

Don't rely only on your index fingers. Train all fingers to "own" specific keys for better efficiency.

6. Practice Every Day

Consistency is key. Practice daily with typing lessons or games to steadily improve.

7. Play Typing Games

Make it fun! Try games like NitroType or ZType.

8. Correct As You Go

Fix mistakes quickly, but don’t overdo it. Avoid staring at the keyboard while correcting.

9. Learn Shortcuts

Master keyboard shortcuts like Ctrl+C (copy), Ctrl+V (paste), Ctrl+Z (undo) to boost productivity.

10. Track Your Progress

Use tools like Monkeytype or TypeRacer to monitor your WPM and accuracy.
